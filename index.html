The problem

The money is out there. Most founders aren't ready to take it.

An investor likes your pitch and asks for the data room. Then the questions start. Who owns what after your SAFEs convert? Why does the model break in year two? Where is the IP assignment from your first developer?

Every unanswered question costs you days. Enough of them and the investor moves to a founder who was ready.

What investors find in an unprepared startup

  • A cap table in a spreadsheet nobody has reconciled against the share register
  • Equity promised to early helpers but never documented
  • A forecast with growth assumptions nobody can explain
  • A valuation picked because it sounded right
  • Contracts, IP and board approvals scattered across inboxes

FAQ

What founders ask first.

Do you guarantee I'll raise money?

No. Investors decide whether to invest. We guarantee the preparation: a complete, diligence-ready company in 21 days, or we work free until it is and refund 20%.

How much of my time will this take?

About 45 minutes a week on calls, plus sending documents when we ask. We do the building.

Are you lawyers?

No. We are fundraising and finance specialists. Where legal documents, restructuring or statutory valuations are needed, licensed partners do that work and we coordinate it.

Do you introduce us to investors?

No. We prepare your company and materials so you are ready when you speak to investors. You run those conversations; Raise-Ready Partner supports you while you do.

We already have a deck and a model. Do we still need the Sprint?

Start with the Audit. If your materials hold up, we'll tell you and you'll only pay for the Audit.

Which countries do you work with?

We are based in Accra and prepare companies for investors in the US, UK, UAE, Singapore, India and Africa.

What stage do you work with?

Pre-seed to Series A. After that, most companies hire a full-time finance lead.

Is my information confidential?

Yes. We sign an NDA before the Audit, and only our team and named partners see your documents.
