Cookie Policy

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. Tags (also called pixels) are snippets of code that send information about your visit to another company. This page lists every one we use. Apart from storing your choice, none of them run until you say yes.

Change my cookie choices

How consent works here

Off until you turn it on.

When you first visit, advertising and measurement are switched off. They load only after you click "Accept all" or turn them on under "Choose". You can change your mind at any time from the "Your privacy choices" link at the bottom of every page.

1. Strictly necessary

Always on, because the site needs it to remember what you chose.

When advertising is on, the tags also send Google and Meta the page you're on, your IP address, browser details and actions such as clicking "Book a call". The Privacy Policy explains how that data is used and shared.

3. Measurement (only with consent, and only if we turn it on)

We don't run Google Analytics today. If we add it, it will appear as a separate "Measurement" switch in the cookie banner. Its cookies are _ga and _ga_* (Google Analytics), which count visits and show which pages are used, and last up to 2 years.

4. Third-party services on our pages

  • Calendly (the booking calendar on the home page) sets its own cookies inside the calendar and shows its own cookie notice. See Calendly's Cookie Notice.
  • Google Fonts serves our fonts. It sets no cookies, but your browser sends your IP address to Google to download them.
  • WhatsApp buttons are plain links. Nothing loads from WhatsApp until you click.

5. Google Consent Mode

We use Google Consent Mode. Before you choose, it tells Google tags that advertising storage, ad user data, ad personalisation and analytics storage are all "denied". Our Google tag doesn't load at all until you consent. If you later withdraw consent, we update these settings, delete the advertising cookies we can reach and reload the page.

6. Other ways to control cookies

  • Use "Your privacy choices" at the bottom of any page.
  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site still works without them.
  • Turn on Global Privacy Control in your browser. We treat it as a "no" to advertising.
  • Manage ads in Google My Ad Center or Meta Ad Preferences.
