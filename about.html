Why I started Investor Ready

In a bank, nothing moves until the numbers reconcile. In a startup, the numbers are always a week behind the business.

I started my first business, Taxido, at 19: an online tax and bookkeeping service. I had more energy than experience, spent too much on ads and made very little. It taught me the lesson every founder learns eventually: the numbers decide whether you survive.

So I went and learned the numbers properly. I audited IIM Rohtak, SBI branches, schools and NGOs. At HSBC I finalised financial statements and ran internal checks for entities across Europe, China, Mexico and the USA, where nothing was signed off until every figure reconciled.

Then I went back to building: Cowsome, Shoogloo, and co-founding Vayoo.store. And I saw the same thing in every startup. Founders are brilliant at building and too busy to keep their paperwork investor-grade, until an investor asks for the data room.

Investor Ready closes that gap: bank-grade checking, done at founder speed, so you walk into diligence with nothing missing.
