# Investor Ready: SEO and Content Plan

Prepared 27 October 2026. Covers the competitor audit, the keyword map, every page the site needs (with what goes on it), the copy approach, and a 12-month roadmap.

> **About keyword data.** No paid keyword tool (DataForSEO, Ahrefs) is connected, so this plan has no measured search volumes. Keywords are chosen from what competitors rank for and target, Google's live results, and buyer intent. Before Phase 2, check volumes for the shortlisted terms in Google Keyword Planner (free) or Ahrefs, and keep the terms that show real demand.

## 2. Competitor audit

| Competitor | What they rank with | Strengths | Gaps we can use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **WaveUp** (waveup.com) | ~40 service pages: one per service (`/financial-modeling-services/`, `/pitch-deck-consulting-services/`, `/due-diligence-consulting/`), one per stage (`/pre-seed-funding-services/`, `/seed-funding-for-startups/`, `/series-a-funding-advisory/`), a blog, a 19-question "VC Quiz" lead magnet, calculators. | Long pages (fundraising page about 4,500 words), case studies with round sizes, big proof numbers ($3B+ raised), 11-question FAQ, comparison tables. | **No guarantee.** Pricing only on request. Duplicate and overlapping pages (two pitch deck pages). Generalist across M&A and design, so not readiness-specific. Its quiz is gated behind an email. |
| **Slidebean** (slidebean.com) | Pitch deck software plus agency. Free financial model template, deck templates, knowledge base, YouTube. | Huge template and tool footprint brings in links and traffic. Trustpilot reviews. | Deck-first; cap table, data room and diligence are thin. Software focus, not done-for-you readiness. |
| **Qubit Capital** (qubit.capital) | Service pages for data room, pitch deck and financial model; industry pages; investor-type pages. | Many URLs per service and industry. | Vague headings ("Neo-networking for neo-business"), no blog or resources, sells investor matching (which we deliberately do not do). |
| **Standard Ledger** (standardledger.co/uk) | One focused page per service, e.g. "Investor-Ready Financial Modelling for Startups". | Clean H1 and title, transparent "from" prices, short FAQ, founder testimonials. | About 1,300 words; little explanation of what goes into a model. UK only. |
| **Visible.vc, Papermark, Carta** (content sites) | Own the informational searches: "data room checklist", "what to include in your data room", "SAFE conversion". | Deep guides and checklists that rank. | They sell software, not done-for-you work. Our guides can win on specificity (stage- and country-specific) and end with a service offer. |
| **Freelancers** (Upwork, Fiverr, Contra) | Rank for "investor ready financial model", "3 statement financial model". | Cheap ($100–$700). | No accountability, no single owner, no guarantee. This is the "hire separate providers" column on our comparison table. |
| **Africa programmes** (GIZ SAIS, VC4A, WDI/Fundable) | Rank for "investment readiness Africa". | Grant-funded, free. | Cohort training, not done-for-you; slow. **Almost no commercial firms rank here.** |

### What this means

1. **Every service needs its own page.** This is how WaveUp, Qubit and Standard Ledger all rank. It is the single biggest change.
2. **Our moat is the offer, not the volume.** No competitor puts a priced, named, guaranteed, fixed-length readiness package on its site. The 21-Day Sprint, published prices and the Diligence-Proof Guarantee are things none of them can easily copy. Lead with them on every page.
3. **Cap table, data room and due diligence are open ground.** Competitors are deck-first or model-first. "Cap table cleanup", "data room for investors" and "due diligence preparation" have few service pages competing; the results are mostly software blogs.
4. **Africa and India are the easiest places to rank first.** We have a real office in Accra. Searches like "investor readiness Ghana", "pitch deck consultant Nigeria" and "Delaware flip India" have weak commercial competition.
5. **Free tools bring links.** Slidebean and WaveUp both use templates, quizzes and calculators. Our Scorecard is ungated, which is an advantage; a SAFE dilution calculator and a data room checklist would follow the same pattern.

Every service page uses the same structure, built from the existing styles:
1. Breadcrumb, H1 with the primary keyword, a lede that states the outcome, and a "What you receive" panel (the same ledger panel as the home hero).
2. The problem: what an investor finds when this file is missing.
3. Educational section (the SEO weight): how the thing works, explained properly. This is the part that ranks and gets cited by AI search.
4. Deliverables, timeline and price, with the Sprint upsell ("included in the Sprint").
5. The guarantee.
6. FAQ, 4–6 real questions.
7. Related services (internal links) and the closing call-to-action band.

## 5. Copy approach (the $100M Offers frameworks)

| Framework | How it's used on the site |
|---|---|
| **Value equation** | Dream outcome: "send the data room link the day an investor asks" and walk into the meeting with a price you can defend. Likelihood: guarantee plus banking and audit track record. Time delay: 21 days, with a red-flag report on day 3 as the first early win. Effort: 45 minutes a week; we do the building. Every hero and service lede covers all four. |
| **Compete on the bottom half** | Competitors make big claims ("$3B raised"). We compete on speed (21 days) and effort (45 minutes a week, done for you), which are harder to copy. |
| **Grand Slam Offer** | The Sprint is the core offer: 8 files plus 4 named bonuses, a $42,000 stated value against an $18,000 price. Price stays far above freelancers on purpose; the comparison table shows why. |
| **Guarantee formula (X, Y, Z)** | "Your data room is complete in 21 days (X, Y), or we keep working free until it is and refund 20% of your fee (Z)." It's conditional on the founder sending documents within 2 business days, which also keeps the Sprint on schedule. Plus the 48-Hour Fix Promise. |
| **Scarcity (real)** | 4 Sprint places a month, because it's true. |
| **Urgency (real)** | The 4 bonuses are included when you book your Audit before the next Sprint start. The cost-of-delay maths makes the deadline concrete. |
| **Naming (M-A-G-I-C)** | "21-Day (interval) Investor-Ready (goal) Sprint (container)"; "Diligence-Proof Guarantee"; "VC Grill" mock sessions. |
| **Lead magnet** | The free Scorecard reveals the problem; the Audit then solves it for a fee that is credited to the Sprint. |
| **Price anchoring** | Show the cost of buying the files separately ($36,500) and the cost of a 3-month delay to runway, before showing $18,000. |

Content rules (unchanged): no investor introductions, never promise a raise, not a law firm, broker-dealer or registered valuer, and no placeholder testimonials.

## 7. Authority and links (off-page)

1. **Directories:** Clutch, GoodFirms, DesignRush (finance consulting), VC4A advisor listing, Startup Grind and local Ghana and Nigeria startup directories.
2. **Founder content:** 3 LinkedIn posts a week drawn from the guides. Under $100M Leads ("give until they ask") these are free content that sends readers to the Scorecard.
3. **Podcasts:** Abhinav as a guest on founder and African-tech podcasts, talking about data rooms and valuations. Each appearance usually earns a backlink.
4. **Partners:** the law and tax firms we coordinate with. Ask for a listing on their partner pages, and offer the same.
5. **Accelerators and hubs:** offer a free "Is your startup investor-ready?" workshop to accelerators (MEST, Ghana Tech Lab, CcHUB, and Indian incubators) in exchange for a resources-page link.
6. **Testimonials and case studies:** after each Sprint, ask for a written testimonial and permission to publish an anonymised case study. These are the strongest missing proof.

**How you'll know it isn't working:** if after 90 days the service pages have been indexed but show under 100 impressions a month in Search Console, the keyword choice or the domain authority is the constraint. Check volumes, then put effort into links before writing more pages.
