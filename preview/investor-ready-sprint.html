Why a Sprint

Getting investor-ready is not hard. It's slow, and it's eight jobs at once.

Every file an investor checks depends on another one. The valuation depends on the model. The model depends on the cap table. The cap table depends on documents your lawyer may never have filed. The deck has to match all three, and the data room has to prove it.

Founders who do this themselves spend two to four months of evenings on it. Founders who hire separately get a deck agency, a freelance modeller, a lawyer and a valuer who never speak to each other, and the numbers in the deck don't match the model. Investors notice. A mismatch between the deck and the data room is one of the first red flags an analyst looks for.

The Sprint fixes the order problem. One team builds all eight files in sequence, so every number matches every other number, and you stay on the business.

Day by day

Three phases. One early win.

  1. Before day 1 · NDA and document request

    We sign an NDA and send one checklist of the documents we need. You send what you have; missing items become part of the fix plan.

  2. Days 1–3 · Audit

    We review your legal records, cap table, accounts, deck and contracts against a standard diligence checklist. On day 3 you get the red-flag report: every gap, graded red, amber or green, in the order we'll fix it. That's your first win.

  3. Days 4–14 · Build

    Structure memo, cap table, model, valuation, deck and data room, built in that order so each file feeds the next. Your lawyer gets a list of any documents that need papering.

  4. Days 15–21 · Stress test and hand-over

    Two "VC Grill" sessions in which we question you the way a partner meeting would. We close the last gaps, then walk you through every file and hand over the data room.

FAQ

Questions about the Sprint.

What is an investor readiness program?

It's the work of preparing a startup for investor due diligence before the raise begins: cleaning the cap table, building the financial model, setting the valuation, writing the pitch deck and assembling the data room. Most programs teach founders to do this themselves over several weeks. The Sprint does it for you in 21 days.

Why does it cost $18,000 when a freelancer will build a model for $500?

A $500 model isn't connected to your cap table, your valuation or your deck, and nobody is accountable when they disagree. The Sprint is eight files built by one team so the numbers match, with a guarantee. It also costs less than buying the same files separately ($36,500), and far less than a raise that stalls in diligence.

What if we already have some of the files?

Start with the Audit. If your deck or model already holds up, we'll tell you, and you'll only pay for the Audit or for the files you actually need.

When does the next Sprint start?

We run 4 Sprints a month so every founder gets senior attention. Book a call and we'll give you the next open start date. Book your Audit before that date and the four bonuses are included.

Do you guarantee we'll raise?

No. Investors decide whether to invest. We guarantee the preparation: complete in 21 days or we work free and refund 20%, and any gap found in diligence fixed within 48 hours for 6 months.
